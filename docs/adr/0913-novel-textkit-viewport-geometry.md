# Share novel typography and viewport geometry without sharing preview state

The [reader design](../architecture/readers.md#小说投影排版与位置) describes the Core-owned generation and its single committed UI TextKit 2 graph. [Display references](../../Sources/YamiboXUI/Features/Reader/Novel/TextKit/NovelTextViewportDisplayReference.swift) hold a weak owner and a full surface identity rather than retaining a copy of the graph. Requests to draw stale surfaces therefore cannot draw the previous document after a layout commit.

[Pagination and clipping](../../Sources/YamiboXUI/Features/Reader/Novel/TextKit/NovelTextKitRuntimeAdapter.swift) use the same document-space line calculation, preserving vertical tolerance and zero-width lines. [The viewport graph](../../Sources/YamiboXUI/Features/Reader/Novel/TextKit/NovelTextKitViewportGraph.swift) uses the same coordinate conversion for selection and viewport sampling without conflating clipping with the nearest-sample fallback. A layout fragment draws its whole paragraph, so gather visible line rectangles and draw once under their combined clip instead of redrawing the paragraph once per visible line.

[Settings preview](../../Sources/YamiboXUI/Features/Reader/Novel/TextKit/NovelTextSettingsPreviewSurface.swift) shares attributed-text styling with the reader without building a viewport index or touching the active workflow. Lay out complete lines past the preview's bounds so the bottom fade can show a partial line. [Leading](../../Sources/YamiboXUI/Features/Reader/Novel/TextKit/NovelAttributedTextFactory.swift) scales with the body size; a fixed increment would be loose at small sizes and cramped at large ones. The user's line-height adjustment still multiplies the result. [Forum-authored colors](../../Sources/YamiboXUI/Features/Reader/Novel/TextKit/NovelForumColorRenderer.swift) are adapted for contrast against the reader's actual paper and quote backgrounds rather than the original forum card.
